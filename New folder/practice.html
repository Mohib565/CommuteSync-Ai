01 / The Classroom Reality

When Concepts Stay Invisible, Interest Fades First

Examine a real classroom scenario: A teacher explains an abstract concept on an ordinary whiteboard using only a marker. Students hear the words, but cannot visualize how the process actually works. While education dropouts have many socio-economic causes, comprehension failure inside the classroom is the foundational trigger.
1. No Visualization Static marker sketches cannot show motion or 3D flow.
2. Hard to Grasp Abstract STEM topics feel confusing and heavy.
3. Lack of Interest Average students disengage and turn to rote memory.
4. Larger Crisis Disengagement compounds Pakistan's dropout risk.
The Teacher's Headache: Schools and colleges strictly teach from fixed textbooks on physical whiteboards. Expecting teachers to manually hunt for verified animations for every daily topic is unrealistic and breaks classroom flow.

02 / Competitor & Market Landscape

Fragmented Globally. Untouched on Pakistani Whiteboards.

Across the world, classroom tools exist in disconnected pieces — hardware in one place, standalone simulations in another. Nobody unites physical whiteboards with real-time AI visuals in Pakistan.

Pakistan Right Now

Student Apps & Digital LMS Only

  • Maqsad, Sabaq, Edkasa: Focus on student mobile apps, recorded lecture videos, and photo-based homework solving.
  • Taleemabad & Knowledge Platform: Build digital LMS and tablet/screen-based lessons.
  • The Gap: Zero live support for teachers teaching on ordinary physical whiteboards with existing projectors.
Global Market (Fragmented)

Scattered Pieces or Costly Hardware

  • Board Cameras (Logitech Scribe, Huddly, Kaptivo): Only digitize/stream board ink to video calls — no topic visuals.
  • Smartboard Hardware (SMART, Promethean, ViewSonic): Require expensive full-board hardware replacement.
  • Simulation Apps (PhET, BioDigital, Khanmigo): Disconnected from physical boards; require manual mid-class searching.
What We Introduce

CommuteSync AI (Unified Layer)

  • No Board Replacement: Software-first layer working on existing whiteboards and projectors.
  • Multimodal Understanding: Combines live whiteboard camera + teacher voice + textbook syllabus context.
  • Hybrid Visual Engine: Verified library first + real-time Agentic AI generation when a topic is unlisted.
03 / Our Solution

Upgrade the Classroom. Don't Replace It.

Multimodal Input (Vision + Voice)

Sees the Whiteboard. Listens to the Teacher.

A camera observes the teacher's natural marker writing while audio context listens to their explanation — resolving shorthand notes, rough sketches, and acting as a fallback in low-light rooms.

Two-Tier Intelligence

Verified Library + Real-Time Agentic AI

Common syllabus topics load instantly from our curated library. If a teacher writes an unlisted concept, our Agentic AI generates an accurate visual live — and caches it for future reuse.

Built for Real Classrooms

Syllabus-Locked, Offline-Capable & Private

Filtered by active subject/chapter, projects cleanly beside handwriting with teacher approval, works offline on preloaded topics, and keeps zero stored audio recordings.

04 / Technical Workflow

Multimodal Detection + Self-Growing Agentic AI

01 • MULTIMODAL INPUT

Board + Voice Capture

USB webcam watches writing zone (ROI); mic listens to teacher explanation (RAM-only, never stored).

02 • AI FUSION

OCR + Speech + Syllabus

PaddleOCR / Vision LLM + Urdu-English ASR fuse with active Level, Subject & Chapter context.

03 • TIER 1 (FAST)

Verified Cloud Library

Vector search retrieves pre-verified visuals from Cloud S3/R2 CDN or local offline campus cache.

04 • TIER 2 (FALLBACK)

Live Agentic AI

On library miss: Prompt Agent LLM crafts structured prompt → Visual API generates → Auto-saves to library.

05 • OUTPUT

Approved Projection

Teacher approves visual; existing projector displays it in a reserved side zone without covering ink.
